Trick or Treat Trail

October 25, 2026

Princesses, pirates, ghosts and goblins are invited to don their costumes and enjoy a spooktacular celebration at the annual Trick or Treat Trail at the Rob Fleming Recreation Center. The FREE event also includes strolling entertainment, carnival games, costume contests, photo areas, food trucks and more!

Kayak Trick-or-Treat

October 25, 2026

Put a spooky spin on Halloween with this on-the-water trick-or-treat adventure! Paddle a tandem kayak through the lower Waterway and Lake Woodlands, stopping at festive stations to collect goodies from friendly staff. Costumes encouraged.
