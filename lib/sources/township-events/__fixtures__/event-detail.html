Flashlight Candy Hunt

Next date: Wednesday, October 28, 2026 | 07:30 PM

Join us for a spooktacular candy hunt at Terramont Park! Dress in your Halloween best, grab your flashlight and bring a trick-or-treat bag or bucket for an evening of sweet treats and festive fun. Costumed encouraged! Prizes will be awarded for best costume and for those that find the hidden Halloween surprise. Parents must accompany children (no charge for adults). Please note: hunts begins at the start time, please be sure to check in by then or you will miss it!
